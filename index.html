Issue and risk assessment

Tip for a useful report: Be factual and specific. Include the person’s description and exact location/carriage, what they are doing or saying, whether weapons, violence, threats, intoxication or injury are involved, who may be at risk, and whether the situation is escalating. State any vulnerabilities, immediate danger, and what assistance you need. Avoid assumptions; distinguish what you saw from what someone told you.
